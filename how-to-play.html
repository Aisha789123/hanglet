How to play

Find the hidden word before the drawing is finished.

The basics

  • The word is shown as empty tiles, one tile per letter. The category above it is your hint.
  • Tap a letter on the screen keyboard, or type it on your own keyboard.
  • A right letter turns green and shows up in every place it appears in the word.
  • A wrong letter turns red and costs one life. Each lost life adds to the drawing.
  • Fill in every letter to win. Run out of lives and the round is over.

Game modes

  • Daily – three words a day (short, medium and long), the same for every player. You can play it once per day. Finish it every day to build your day streak.
  • Classic – endless play. Choose a category, word length and difficulty.
  • Levels – 40 levels in four stages. Words get longer and you get fewer lives. Win a level to unlock the next. Zero or one miss gives three stars.
  • Time Attack – solve as many words as you can in 2 minutes. A missed word just moves you on to the next one.

Options

  • Word length: short (4–5 letters), medium (6–7) or long (8 or more).
  • Difficulty: easy (8 lives), normal (6 lives) or hard (4 lives).
  • Categories: animals, food, countries, sports, nature, jobs, science and music, or Mixed for all of them.

Rewards

  • Every win gives XP and coins. Fewer misses and harder games give more.
  • XP raises your player level. New levels unlock new color themes.
  • Spend 20 coins on a hint to reveal a letter. A hint never reveals the last letter.
  • Collect badges for things like your first win, a game without misses or a 7-day streak. Every badge gives 25 coins.

Keyboard

  • A–Z guesses a letter.
  • Tab and Enter move through and press buttons, like "Next word".
