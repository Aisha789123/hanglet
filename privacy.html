Privacy

Short version: your data stays on your device.

What is stored

Hanglet saves your progress in your browser's localStorage: your settings, statistics, streaks, coins, XP, level stars and badges. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

What is not collected

  • No account, name or email address.
  • No analytics, tracking cookies or advertising.
  • No location or other personal data.

Fonts

The Nunito font is loaded from Google Fonts. Like any website, Google can see the request for the font file. After the first visit the font is saved for offline use.

Sharing results

The share button only copies a short text to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you paste it somewhere yourself.

Removing your data

Go to Settings and choose Reset progress, or clear the site data in your browser settings.
