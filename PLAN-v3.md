# Hanglet v3 – from app to game

Goal: a fun, playful, "one more round" word game with its own original look.
Work through the phases in this order. Tick the boxes when done.

## Design direction (own style)
- Mascot **Tilo**: a round yellow letter tile with a little hook on top (hang + letter), big eyes, rosy cheeks
- Logo: the word HANGLET built from colored letter tiles with depth
- Fonts: **Fredoka** (round, sturdy) for headings, tiles and buttons + **Nunito** for text
- Palette: sky blue, sunshine yellow, coral, mint, berry, grape-free (no purple gradients)
- Soft illustrated background: blobs, a light tile pattern, depth; deep navy in dark mode
- Chunky 3D buttons and tiles that press down
